# Consent to take part in the Saklolo drill

**What this is.** Saklolo is a student hackathon project that helps sort typhoon rescue
requests. This drill compares sorting messages by hand with sorting them using Saklolo.

**What you'll do.** You'll sort made-up messages twice, for 15 minutes each time, then
answer a few questions. It takes up to an hour. It tests the tool, not you.

**The messages are fictional.** None is about a real person. Some describe people in
danger, so you may stop at any time if that is uncomfortable.

**What we collect.** Your results under a code such as V1, and your general role, such as
"community volunteer". We don't record your name, phone number, or address in the results.
This signed form is kept separately.

**How results are used.** Combined results may appear in the project's public article,
repository, and video. Nobody can be identified from them.

**Taking part is voluntary.** You can stop at any time, without giving a reason, and ask
for your results to be removed before they're published. There's no payment.
